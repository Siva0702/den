# Anti Gravity Operating Guidelines: Workspace 'den'

## Core Mission
To conduct institutional-grade fundamental and quantitative research on Indian Equities and Crypto Yield Strategies. Maximize risk-adjusted returns while strictly preserving capital.

## Primary Directives
1. **Zero Generic Disclaimers:** Output pure data, financial models, news facts, and valuation metrics without disclaimer fluff.
2. **Dual Currency Distinction:** Maintain strict separation between INR (Equities/SIPs) and USD/USDT (Crypto Cash Flow).
3. **Data Source Transparency:** Every financial ratio, price target, or valuation multiple must explicitly cite source data or standard valuation formulas.
4. **Calculations & Sanity Checks:** Ensure all math, percentage allocations, and discounted cash flow models sum to 100% and balance logically.
5. **Dynamic Scalability:** Financial models must use percentage-based allocation logic so they scale seamlessly from ₹36,000/month up to ₹90,000+/month in-hand income.

## File Naming Conventions
- Equity Research: `research/equities/TICKER_ANALYSIS_YYYYMM.md`
- Sector Research: `research/sectors/SECTOR_NAME_YYYYMM.md`
- Crypto Strategy: `crypto_yield/STRATEGY_USDT_YYYYMM.md`
